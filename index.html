The Estate

So private and picturesque that it feels like living at a resort.

Three Gated Acres in Potomac

Set on three lush, gated acres in Potomac, 9325 Belle Terre Way is so private and picturesque that it feels like living at a resort. The estate includes a main house of approximately 8,400 square feet, the five-bedroom Rose Cottage, a pool and pool house, a sport court, and a potting shed. The current owners took the main house down to the studs and rebuilt it from the inside out, with exceptional attention to every exquisite detail.

The Main House

The Arrival

Enter the main house through an arched steel-and-glass front door into a two-story foyer with a curved staircase.

The Speakeasy

The living room has been reimagined as a speakeasy, with deep green walls, a marble-topped bar framed by brass and glass shelving, billiards, a gas fireplace, and a hidden door to the family room.

The Family Room

A coffered ceiling, built-ins, and another gas fireplace give the family room its inviting character, while French doors open to the stone patio, continuing the effortless indoor/outdoor flow throughout the main level.

The Dining Room

The dining room sits beneath a tray ceiling and connects to the kitchen through a beautiful butler’s pantry.

The Kitchen

The 32-foot eat-in kitchen centers on a waterfall marble island and features a La Cornue range beneath a custom hood, two dishwashers, a wine refrigerator, separate refrigerator and freezer columns, walnut glass-front cabinetry, and a walk-in pantry.

The Screened Porch

A room for every season.

The screened porch is a room for every season, with retractable screens, a wood-burning fireplace, built-in heaters, ceiling fans, and a built-in grill.

Upstairs

The Primary Suite

Upstairs, the primary suite is a retreat of its own, featuring a sitting area with a gas fireplace and two beautifully appointed dressing rooms. One dressing room includes a center island and opens to a covered balcony.

The Primary Bath

The spectacular marble bathroom offers a freestanding tub, twin vanities, and a gorgeous shower with three shower heads.

The Upper Levels

Three additional bedrooms each have their own en-suite bath and walk-in closets; two occupy their own wing with a sitting area. A beautifully appointed laundry room with two sets of washers and dryers completes the level.

On the third floor, a skylit fifth bedroom suite or office is lined with built-ins and has a full bath of its own.

The Lower Level

Theater and Wine Room

The lower level features a home theater with a bar area, a glass-enclosed wine room, a bedroom with a full bath, and an office with a built-in desk and shelving.

A three-car garage and a mudroom with built-in lockers are conveniently located on this level.

The Grounds

The grounds have been completely relandscaped, with 80 new trees, new beds, a perimeter fence, irrigation, and landscape lighting throughout. A new stone pathway winds from the back of the house down to the pool, set on its own level of the property. This thoughtful placement creates a true retreat while allowing the landscaped grounds to take center stage in views from the house.

The Pool and Pool House

The 62-foot pool has been rebuilt with a new deck, waterline tile, lights, heater, pump, Wi-Fi controls, and cover. Beside it, the heated and air-conditioned pool house features a kitchenette, bath, and changing room.

Rose Cottage

The outdoors are just as extraordinary. A stone path leads to Rose Cottage, a fully refurbished guest house completed in 2023 and large enough for a whole family to stay. It offers five bedrooms, two baths, a kitchen, a living room with a gas fireplace, and a finished lower level with a family room and laundry. A new boiler, central air on a Nest system, new windows and doors, and its own patio complete the cottage.

The Sport Court

Follow the path below to a tucked-away sport court, redone in 2023. A restored potting shed with attic storage and its own patio completes the estate.

Exquisitely Transformed

Every day feels like a vacation.

9325 Belle Terre Way has been exquisitely transformed. The result is a rare private estate where every day feels like a vacation, just minutes from everything Potomac has to offer.
